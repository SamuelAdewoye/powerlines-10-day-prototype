# Powerlines — Design Exploration

## Three possible approaches

### 1. Editorial Redline
**Very Brief Intro:** A sharp, print-derived interface built from wide margins, red rules, decisive typography, and deliberate pacing. It feels like opening a marked manifesto rather than joining a wellness product.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Quiet Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, paper-led practice space with restrained annotation cues and measured, reflective rhythm. It would emphasize private observation over confrontation.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Concrete Ritual
**Very Brief Intro:** A brutalist ritual log inspired by institutional forms, hard edges, and repetitive daily evidence. It would make every response feel documented and accountable.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach: Editorial Redline

### Design Movement
Editorial Redline draws on contemporary editorial design and marked-up manifestos. It translates the journal’s authority into a digital reading room: print-like, uncompromising, and intentionally sparse.

### Core Principles
1. **Authority before friendliness:** Every screen states a clear idea. No celebratory language, gamification, or comfort-first interaction patterns.
2. **Negative space is active:** Wide margins and measured line lengths create attention for the Secret and for the reader’s answer.
3. **Linear practice, not content browsing:** The day moves in one direction—Secret, Story, Lessons, Diagnostic, Power Move, Completion.
4. **Visible commitment:** The only decisive action is the commitment to today’s Power Move; progress is evidence, not a reward loop.

### Color Philosophy
Sovereign Black is the field of seriousness, used for containment and decisive actions. Declaration White is the reading surface, signalling clarity rather than softness. Reclaim Red is a structural mark: it denotes the line being drawn, the choice being made, and the element requiring attention. Red appears as rules, active indicators, and commitment surfaces—not as decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
The experience uses an asymmetric editorial spread. A thin black rail carries the day position and exit route; the reading surface is a wide, off-white field. On desktop, the Secret occupies a broad left field with an intentional red vertical rule; the working screens place the response area beside the prompt, avoiding a centered dashboard or card grid. On mobile, the same elements stack without losing their hierarchy.

### Signature Elements
1. **The Redline:** a thin vertical or horizontal Reclaim Red rule, used to mark the primary principle or action.
2. **The Position Index:** `DAY 03 / 10` appears in small, tightly tracked Poppins type on every screen.
3. **The Closing Bar:** a solid black or red commitment strip only where the user is asked to proceed or mark a Power Move.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are purposeful and immediate. Buttons are large, plain-language directives such as `CONTINUE`, `SAVE RESPONSE`, and `MARK AS COMMITTED`. There is no swiping theatre, no engagement score, and no content recommendations. The current day is unlocked by default for demonstration; the next day is only offered after today’s commitment is recorded.

### Animation
No motion is used in the daily experience. Screen changes occur instantly. Hover and active states use an immediate color inversion or rule shift only, and all controls respect reduced-motion preferences by avoiding transitions.

### Typography System
**Bebas Neue** carries headlines, day numbers, and the Secret. It is set large, uppercase, and compact to feel declarative. **Poppins** carries narrative, questions, responses, labels, and timestamps. Poppins is used at a calm reading size with relaxed leading; no alternate display font is introduced.

### Brand Essence
**A 30-day digital practice for young adults who want to reclaim their attention, direct their choices, and build a life that feels authored.**

Personality: **clear, rigorous, self-possessed.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines state a principle or a decision. CTAs name the action without pushing. Microcopy treats the reader as capable and already responsible for the next move.

Example headline: `YOUR ATTENTION IS A CHOICE WITH CONSEQUENCES.`

Example CTA: `NAME THE NEXT MOVE.`

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark is a compact Bebas Neue `P/` monogram paired with `POWERLINES` in uppercase. The slash acts as the redline: a simple graphic interruption rather than an ornamental icon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Reclaim Red `#C0001A`** is the unmistakable Powerlines mark.

## Style Decisions

- Daily screens must remain illustration-free and free of social-feed, gamification, recommendation, or notification patterns.
- The Secret is always the first, largest element of a day.
- Every design choice must answer: *Does this add clarity or merely add interface?*
- Reclaim Red `#C0001A` functions as a rule, interruption, active index, or commitment surface—never as atmospheric decoration.
- The welcome screen may use Sovereign Black as its primary field, but all reading, story, lesson, and diagnostic screens use Declaration White as the primary surface.
- Copy must sound like a marked principle with consequences, never a generic productivity challenge or motivational slogan.
